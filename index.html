Billion Dollar Disasters

Original short documentaries about the business stories behind the biggest failures — companies, products and deals in technology, finance, media, cars and other industries. Every video is researched from published sources, listed in its description, and uses licensed or public-domain images.

BDD Publisher

BDD Publisher is an internal tool used only by the channel owner to publish and schedule videos on his own YouTube channel and to read that channel's analytics. It has no other users, no public sign-in and no user interface. It uses the YouTube Data API, the YouTube Analytics API and the YouTube Reporting API.

  • Uploads finished videos that the channel owner has approved, with title, description, tags, thumbnail, captions and playlist, scheduled for a set publish time.
  • Posts YouTube Shorts made from those videos.
  • Reads views, watch time and audience-retention figures for the owner's own channel to improve future videos.
